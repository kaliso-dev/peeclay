About

A sculptural residence that rises out of the ridgeline, where concrete, glass and the canyon meet to shape a calmer way of living.

Designed by architect Jeffrey Eyster, AIA, for developer Tim McElwee and completed in 2026, the house is cast from some ten million pounds of reinforced concrete: cantilevered volumes, long open spans and angled columns that frame the view.

Inside, board-formed concrete ceilings, warm walnut and polished terrazzo carry one material language from room to terrace, behind walls of glass and floor-to-ceiling pivot doors.
